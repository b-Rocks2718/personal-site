"A monad is just a monoid in the category of endofunctors, what's the problem?"

Many people have heard this phrase, but even after learning how to use monads in
a functional language like Haskell, it might not be clear what it means. 
In this post, I'll unpack this statement and explain the connection between
Haskell and the category theory behind it.
