In this article I'll be giving a brief introduction to homology,
a tool from algebraic topology used for classifying topological spaces.
I was inspired to write this because it seems like there are a lot of great
resources explaining the fundamental group[^homotopy-sources], but compartively few resources 
explaining homology in an accessible way. This surprises me because
homology gives you similar information to homotopy groups, but
is often much easier to work with. If you have a good understanding of linear algebra,
then you have everything you need to start computing homology groups!

## Simplicial Complexes

There are multiple kinds of homology, but I'll just be covering the simplest one: simplicial homology. 
Simplicial homology gets its name from because it works by dividing a 
topological space into simplices, which are the higher-dimensional 
analogues of triangles and tetrahedra 
(a triangle is a 2-simplex, a tetrahedron is a 3-simplex).

## Simplicial Homology
